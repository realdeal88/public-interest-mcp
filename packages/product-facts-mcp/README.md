# Product Facts MCP

Search food products and inspect their declared ingredients, allergens, and nutrition facts with [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/). It supports consumers, retailers, educators, and nutrition-aware households.

## Tools

- `search_products` — search the international product catalog by text.
- `get_product` — look up a product by barcode.

Product records are community-supplied and may be incomplete or outdated. Always check the physical package for allergens, ingredients, and medical dietary decisions.
